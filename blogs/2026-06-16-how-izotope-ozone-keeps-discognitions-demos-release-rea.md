For Denver-based DJ and producer Discognition, whose recent remix of Eli & Fur's "Air" landed on NYX Music, finishing records became a matter of speed, trust, and context. He uses iZotope Ozone to quickly get a release-ready loudness and tonal balance and to evaluate works-in-progress without long revision cycles, relying on its visual feedback to catch issues like heavy low-end or masked vocals.
